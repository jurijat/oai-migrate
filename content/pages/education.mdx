The OpenAPI Initiative is currently creating a training course entitled “OpenAPI Fundamentals”. The material created will be available for training providers as an open source suite of modules to include in their curriculum. OpenAPI is seeking feedback on the first two modules. If interested, click on the buttons below which will take you to the education project on GitHub.

The OpenAPI Initiative is committed to investing resources to grow the community, continuing to extend the technology, and providing a collaboration hub for sharing and best practices.

[Project Repository](https://github.com/OAI/OAI-Courses)[How to give feedback](https://github.com/OAI/OAI-Courses/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
