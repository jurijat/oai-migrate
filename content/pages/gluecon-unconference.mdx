The OpenAPI Initiative [Technical Steering Committee](/about/technical-steering-committee) invites you to a half-day [unconference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unconference) at [Gluecon](http://gluecon.com/) on May 21 from 1-6pm at the Omni Interlocken Resort in Broomfield, Colorado!

Being an unconference, you the participants get to vote on what we’ll cover — and anything from where the spec is going to problems implementing it to new features you’d like to see is fair game. 🙂 Submit the topics that most interest you now, or bring a list with you to the event.

We will self-organize at 1pm sharp so be sure to get there on time. Plenty of coffee will be on hand, and after the meeting we’ll gather for beverages and networking in the [hotel taproom](https://www.omnihotels.com/hotels/denver-interlocken/dining/tap-room-terrace-restaurant).

Registration is free but space is limited, so reserve your spot today!
