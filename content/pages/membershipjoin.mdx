As a Linux Foundation Collaborative Project, the OpenAPI Initiative aims to create an open source, technical community where industry participants may easily contribute to and adopt the project’s technology and focus on creating, evolving and promoting a vendor neutral description format known as the OpenAPI Specification _(formerly known as the Swagger Specification)_.

The OpenAPI Initiative is committed to creating a structure to invest resources in growing the community, continuing to extend the technology, and providing a collaboration hub for sharing and best practices.

## Membership Details

| Participation Level \* | Fee to Join | Board Seat | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Participating Member | See table below | Yes | Includes sponsorship discounts to OpenAPI hosted events |
| Associate Member | $0 | Special restricted seat | Restricted to pre-approved open source projects, non-governmental organizations, academic institutions, or government research institutions. |

**All member companies must also be a member of The Linux Foundation, unless there are less than 10 employees. Please visit [https://www.linuxfoundation.org/about/join](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/about/join) to learn more about Linux Foundation membership.**

The annual fee for Participating Membership is determined according to the following table, based on your current consolidated employee headcount:

| Employees at Company | Not Yet an LF Member | Existing LF Member |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5,000 or more | $45,000 | $25,000 |
| 500-4,999 | $30,000 | $15,000 |
| 100-499 | $20,000 | $10,000 |
| 10-99 | $10,000 | $5,000 |
| Less than 10 | $1,000 | $500 |
